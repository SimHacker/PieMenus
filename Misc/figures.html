Figures from Interaction and Context in Zoomable User Interfaces

These are some of the figures from Stuart Pook's doctoral thesis, titled ``Interaction and Context in Zoomable User Interfaces'', defended on 15 June 2001. Some of them may have to be cropped to get exactly the figure shown in the thesis.

These figures are available PDF and Encapsulated Postscript format so that they can included in articles. Please do not publish the PNG bitmap images in articles.
